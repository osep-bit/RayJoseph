About Me

I don't just manage products.
I build them.

Hi — I'm Ray. I'm a product and delivery leader who has spent over a decade in the UAE turning complex digital ideas into platforms that millions of people actually use. My work sits at the intersection of product strategy, technical delivery, and business growth — I'm equally comfortable in a boardroom presenting to C-level executives and in a sprint planning session with engineers.

My flagship product, GameCentric, is a Gaming Ecosystem-as-a-Service I architected from scratch at Iconic Technologies. It's a live, multi-region B2B2C platform with 200,000+ users, a built-in fintech payout layer, and a white-label engine used by global enterprise brands. It's the product I'm most proud of — and the one that best shows how I think.

Beyond GameCentric, I've delivered custom digital platforms for Emirates NBD, Dubai Esports Festival, Riyadh Season, BMW, Mastercard, Kaspersky, LIV Golf, Vodacom, and more. Every project involves its own blend of stakeholder complexity, technical challenge, and delivery pressure — and I thrive in that environment.

I'm based in Dubai, open to senior product and delivery leadership roles across fintech, gaming, enterprise tech, and any company serious about building digital platforms that last.
